# Defensive workflow

Scope the report to one run and verify the audit chain. Summarize the incident timeline, findings, proposed and performed actions, rollback outcomes, measured results, service impact, gaps, provenance, and follow-ups. Do not invent ground truth or metrics. Redact sensitive data and cite event IDs.
